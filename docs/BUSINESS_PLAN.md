## 1. The Problem: Why Getting a Visa is Broken

Every year, hundreds of millions of people apply for international visas.
Almost all of them go through the same painful, frustrating experience:

| The Pain | What Actually Happens |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Confusion** | People don't know if they even need a visa. They Google it and get outdated, wrong information. |
| **Complex Forms** | Official government application forms are long, confusing, and in foreign languages. A single typo causes rejection. |
| **Wrong Documents** | Embassies have hidden document requirements. People show up with the wrong papers and are turned away. |
| **Bank Statement Panic** | Applicants don't know if their finances meet the embassy's threshold. They only find out after rejection. |
| **No Appointments** | VFS Global and US Consulates are booked for months. People spend hours refreshing screens hoping for a cancellation. |
| **Expensive Agents** | Traditional visa agents charge $100–$500, are slow, make mistakes, and are only available in big cities. |
| **eVisa Portal Frustration** | Government eVisa portals are ugly, time out, only accept foreign credit cards, and fail mid-process. |

**The result:** Rejected visas, missed vacations, failed business trips, wasted money, and anxiety.

## 4. The Two Core Product Tracks

When a user enters their passport country and destination, Torii automatically detects which of two tracks applies and adjusts the entire workflow.

---

### Track A — The "Sit Back & Relax" Track (eVisa / Digital Entry)
**For:** Countries where no physical embassy visit is needed.  
**Examples:** Turkey, Vietnam, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Kenya, Malaysia, Cambodia, Bahrain, Qatar, Jordan, Sri Lanka, Rwanda.

**What the user does:**
1. Upload passport photo and answer simple questions.
2. Pay on Torii (local payment methods accepted).
3. Wait for an email. That's it.

**What Torii does behind the scenes:**
1. AI validates the passport (expiry, blank pages, photo compliance).
2. Stealth browser automation logs into the official government eVisa portal.
3. All form fields are filled automatically with the user's verified data.
4. A virtual corporate card (Stripe Issuing) pays the government visa fee.
5. The approved eVisa PDF is downloaded and sent to the user's dashboard and email.

**Customer effort:** Near zero.  
**Torii staff needed:** **Zero humans.**  
**Delivery time:** 5 minutes to 24 hours (depending on the government's processing speed).

---

### Track B — The "Walk In Ready" Track (In-Person Embassy / VFS)
**For:** Countries that legally require the applicant to appear in person for biometric fingerprinting.  
**Examples:** Schengen (Germany, France, Italy), USA B1/B2, United Kingdom, Canada, Japan, Australia.

**Important legal reality:** No agent can legally provide fingerprints on behalf of a traveler. The applicant must appear in person — this is a biometric law, not a process choice. But Torii makes that 10-minute appointment the **only** thing they have to do.

**What Torii delivers:**

| Step | What Torii Delivers |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. AI Document Audit** | Checks every uploaded document against embassy standards. Flags issues before booking. |
| **2. Auto-Filled Official Forms** | Generates the exact government PDF application form, perfectly filled, with no typos. |
| **3. Cover Letter** | Writes a personalized, professional letter explaining the applicant's travel purpose, finances, and ties to home country. |
| **4. Travel Itinerary** | Generates a credible, day-by-day itinerary matching hotel and flight bookings. |
| **5. Financial Solvency Check** | Calculates if the bank balance meets the embassy's daily spending requirement. |
| **6. Slot Sniper** | Monitors VFS / consulate appointment calendars 24/7. Alerts or auto-books the moment a slot opens. |
| **7. Printable Dossier** | Outputs one single, perfectly organized, numbered print-ready PDF packet with exact VFS instructions. |
| **8. Interview Prep Kit** | For USA/UK/Canada: generates the top questions the officer will ask and recommended answers based on the user's profile. |

**Customer effort:** Upload documents, answer questions, print the packet, show up for the appointment.  
**Torii staff needed:** **Zero humans.**

## 5. Document Concierge Services — The Documents Users Cannot Get Alone

This is a **critical gap** that no visa platform has solved properly.

When a user applies for a medical visa to India (or any specialty visa), the embassy does not just want a filled form — they want proof that a **real hospital, real doctor, and real institution** have acknowledged the applicant. Obtaining these documents is the hardest part of the entire process, and it is the #1 reason applications stall before they even begin.

Torii will offer a **Document Concierge** add-on service that procures or generates these supporting documents on the user's behalf.

---

### 5A. Hospital Appointment Letter (Medical Visas)
**The Problem:**  
For India Medical Visa (MED-1), Thailand Medical Visa, Germany, Singapore medical travel — the embassy **mandates** an official invitation letter from the destination hospital on hospital letterhead, stating the patient's name, passport number, diagnosis, treatment plan, and doctor's registration number.

Most patients don't know how to contact Apollo Hospitals, Fortis, or Medanta in India from Bangladesh or Nigeria. They don't know which department to call, what to request, or how long it takes.

**Torii's Solution:**
- Torii maintains a **partner network of recognized hospitals** in India (Apollo, Fortis, Medanta, Max, CMC Vellore), Thailand (Bumrungrad), Turkey (Memorial, Acibadem), and Germany.
- When the user selects their destination hospital and treatment category, Torii's system **automatically sends a standardized appointment request** to the hospital's international patient department on the user's behalf.
- The hospital issues the official letter directly to Torii, which is then delivered to the user's dashboard.
- **Timeline:** 24–72 hours.
- **Price:** $29–$49 add-on fee (Torii keeps a referral/coordination fee; the hospital may also pay Torii a referral commission for the patient).

> **Double revenue model:** Torii charges the patient a coordination fee AND earns a hospital referral commission. This makes the hospital letter add-on potentially the **highest-margin product in the entire platform**.

---

### 5B. Home Country Doctor Referral Letter
**The Problem:**  
For medical visas, embassies require a letter from a licensed physician in the **applicant's home country** confirming the diagnosis and recommending treatment abroad. Many patients:
- Have no regular doctor
- Have a doctor but don't know how to request a formal referral letter in English
- Have a doctor whose letter is not in the correct embassy-accepted format

**Torii's Solution:**
- **Option 1 — AI-Assisted Letter Generator:** The user describes their condition and uploads any existing medical reports. Torii generates a professionally structured, embassy-compliant referral letter template. The user takes this to their local doctor to sign and stamp — reducing the doctor's work to 30 seconds.
- **Option 2 — Telemedicine Referral Partnership:** Torii partners with licensed telemedicine platforms (e.g., local MDs on call). The user does a 15-minute video consultation, and the licensed doctor issues a formal referral letter. Delivered within 24 hours.
- **Price:** $19–$39 add-on fee.

---

### 5C. Dummy Flight Reservation (Proof of Onward Travel)
**The Problem:**  
Embassies require proof of a return/onward flight ticket. But buying a real ticket before the visa is approved is risky — if the visa is rejected, the applicant loses the ticket money.

**Torii's Solution:**  
Torii partners with flight reservation services to issue a **real PNR (Passenger Name Record) reservation** from the airline — verifiable on the airline's website — held for 24–72 hours without actual payment. This is 100% legal and embassy-accepted.
- **Price:** $9–$15 add-on fee.

---

### 5D. Travel Insurance Certificate
**The Problem:**  
Schengen visas **legally require** travel insurance with a minimum €30,000 medical coverage. UK and Canada also strongly prefer it. Most applicants don't know which policies are embassy-accepted, and buying insurance before knowing the visa is approved feels like a risk.

**Torii's Solution:**  
Torii partners with insurance providers (AXA, Allianz, or a regional insurer) to offer **Torii-issued travel insurance** directly at checkout — one click, instant certificate, 100% Schengen-compliant. The user pays for only the dates they need.
- **Price:** $12–$35 (Torii earns a broker/referral commission from the insurer — no upfront cost to build).

---

### 5E. Hotel Booking Confirmation (Proof of Accommodation)
**The Problem:**  
Embassies require confirmed hotel bookings. But many hotels charge upfront or are non-refundable. Applicants hesitate to book hotels before their visa is approved.

**Torii's Solution:**  
Torii partners with Booking.com or a hotel reservation API to issue **free-cancellation hotel confirmation letters** that are valid for 48–72 hours and 100% embassy-accepted, at no financial risk to the user.
- **Price:** $0 (Torii earns affiliate commission from the hotel booking platform).

---

### 5F. Sponsorship / Invitation Letter (for Visiting Family or Friends)
**The Problem:**  
Many visa applications require a formal invitation letter from a host in the destination country — a family member, employer, or conference organizer. The letter must be in a specific format, notarized in some cases, and include the host's ID and address.

**Torii's Solution:**  
Torii generates a **legally formatted invitation letter template** that the host can fill in and sign in under 5 minutes, with exact embassy language and all required fields. For countries requiring notarization, Torii guides the host to the nearest notary or online notarization service.
- **Price:** $14 add-on fee.
